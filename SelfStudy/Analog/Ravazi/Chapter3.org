 -> For this circuit we can make the conclusion that as \(V_{in}\)approaches \(V_{th}\) M1 begins to turn on
  or enter the saturation region. This action draws current from \(R_{D}\) subsequently lowering \(V_{out}\)
  + This leaves us with this equation \(V_{out} = V_{DD} - R_{D} \frac{1}{2} \mu_{n} C_{ox} \frac{W}{L}(V_{in} - V_{TH})^{2}\)
    * Here channel length modulation is neglected
    * The transistor is able to operate in saturation until \(V_{in} > V_{out}\)
  + Recall that the *Triode Current* is  \(I_{d} = \mu_{n}C_{ox}\frac{w}{L}[(V_{GS}-V_{TH})V_{DS}-\frac{V_{DS}^{2}}{2}]\)
