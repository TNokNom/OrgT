* Overview
** Reference Circuit
+ The Idea behind biasing comes from the inherent non-uniformity in semiconductor manufacturing process 
  * Where physical variations & mismatches are inevitable 
+ Reference circuits are anything that provides an absolute reference 
  * as stated above these circuits are not easy to design due to the reasons stated above 
    * It becomes a difficult task to estimate the necessary variables especially when accounting for the constant changes in the semiconductor manufacturing process 
+ Since looking for thing in an IC that change a lot is difficult especially in the analog domain
  * The main one used is the bandgap oscilkit
+ Absolute references take the ideology stated above to the next step
   * They establish some variable (Voltage,Current, or time) that remains immune to fluctuations in semiconductor processing,supply voltages, temperature, etc.
