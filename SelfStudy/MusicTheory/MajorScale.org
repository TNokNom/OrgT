** Understanding Intervals
+ Major scales follow a pattern of intervals
  * An interval is the gap between two notes
  * The interval between two adjacent notes is called the *semitone*
+ In our Major C Scale there is a total of 12 semitones
  * The gap between C and D has a black key in between this means there is two semitones between the two notes
     and a gap of two is called a *Tone*
+ Each major scales follows this format
  * WWHWWWH    W being a Whole Gap and H being a Half Gap
