#+title: Discrete Time Signals

+ A *discrete-time signal*: a sequence of complex numbers
  * One dimension
  * notation: \(x[n]\)
  * Two sided sequence \(x: Z \rightarrow C\)
  * n is a-dimensional "time"
* Application

** Synthesis Types
+ FM synthesis
+ Subtractive Synthesis
+ Physical Modeling
+ Waveshaping
+ Spectral Synthesis
+ Additive Synthesis
