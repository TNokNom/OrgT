* Rotational Motion
+ Here we discuss speed and its various variables
  * \(\omega_{m}  \) angular veloctiy expressed in radians per second
  * \(f_{m}\) = angular veloctiy expressed in revolutions per second
  * \(n_{m} \) angular velocity expressed in revolutions per minute
+ Angular acceleration is the rate of change in angular veloctiy with respect to time
  * \(\alpha = \frac{d\omega}{dt}\)
+ Torque is the measure of a force that can cause an object to rotate about an axis
  * \(\tau = (rsin\theta)F\)
+ Moment Of Inertia is the measure of an object's resistance to changes in its rotational motion
  * \(I = mr^{2}\) for a point mass
+ Work is defined for linear motion, as the application of a force through a distance
  * \(W = \int F \cdot dr \rightarrow W = Fr\)
+ Or for rotational motion
  * \( W = \int \tau \cdot d\theta \rightarrow W = \tau\theta\)
+ And for Power
  * \(P = \tau \omega\)
+ And
  * \( W = \tau \cdot I\) I being moment of inertia
  * \( t = \frac{\omega}{\alpha} \)  t time

  * \(\alpha = \frac{\tau}{I}\)
