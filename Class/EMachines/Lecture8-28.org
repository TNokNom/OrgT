* Magnetic Field Principles
1. When a current passes through a wire, it produces a magnetic field in its vicinity
2. A time-changing magnetic field induces a voltage in a coil of wire if it passes through that coil
3. When a current passes through a wire in the presence of a magnetic field, it has
    a force induced on it (*Motor*)
4. When a wire is moving in the presence of a magnetic field, it has a voltage induced in it
+ In a core the flux density is given by
 * \(\phi = \int_{A}{B \cdot dA}\)
  * With dA being the differential area

+ A magnetic field can be generated by a current
  * We know this through *Ampere's law* \(\oint{H \cdot dl} = I_{net}\)
[[file:../../../MacShots/Magnetic_Field_Principles/2025-08-28_18-50-32_screenshot.png]]
 * H is the magnetic field
 * dl is the differential element of length along the path of integration
+ From this we cant derive these two equation

  -> \(Hl_{c} = Ni\)

  -> \(H = \frac{Ni}{l_{c}\)

  + The relationship between the magnetic field and the resulting magnetic flux density produced within
    a material is given by:
 * \(B = \mu H\)
