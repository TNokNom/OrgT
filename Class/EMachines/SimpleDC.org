* Linear DC Machine
+ Linear DC machines are the simplest dc machines
  * They operate around the same principles as real generators and motors
  * Are used as example study of machines
** Equations
 + We can model the behavior of this simple machine with these four equations
   + \(F = i(l \times B)\)
     * F = force on the wire
     * i =  magnitude of current in the wire
     * L = length of wire, with direction of I defined to be  in the direction of current flow
     * B = Magnetic flux density vector
 + \( e_{ind} = (v \times B) \cdot l\)
   * eind = voltage induced in wire
   * v = velocity of the wire
   * B = magnetic flux density vector
   * l = length of conductor in the magnetic field
 + Newtons law for the bar across the tracks
   * \(F_{net} = ma\)
