* AC CIRCUIT ANALYSIS

** Impedance and Phasors
- Impedance:
  \( Z = R + jX \)
  \( |Z| = \sqrt{R^2 + X^2} \)
  \( \theta = \tan^{-1}(X/R) \)

- Reactances:
  \( X_L = \omega L \)
  \( X_C = -\frac{1}{\omega C} \)

- Current:
  \( I = \frac{V}{Z} \)

- Power factor:
  \( \text{PF} = \cos(\theta) \)

- Phasor RMS:
  \( V_{\text{rms}} = \frac{V_m}{\sqrt{2}} \)

** AC Power
- Real power:
  \( P = VI \cos\theta \)

- Reactive power:
  \( Q = VI \sin\theta \)

- Apparent:
  \( S = P + jQ = VI^* \)
  \( |S| = \sqrt{P^2 + Q^2} \)

** Three-Phase Systems (Balanced)
- Power:
  \( P_{3\phi} = \sqrt{3} V_L I_L \cos\theta \)
  \( Q_{3\phi} = \sqrt{3} V_L I_L \sin\theta \)
  \( S_{3\phi} = \sqrt{3} V_L I_L \)

- Y/Δ Relations:
  - Y:
    \( V_\phi = \frac{V_L}{\sqrt{3}} \)
    \( I_\phi = I_L \)
  - Δ:
    \( V_\phi = V_L \)
    \( I_\phi = \frac{I_L}{\sqrt{3}} \)

** Complex Power Using Phasors
If:
\( V = V_m \angle \alpha \)
\( I = I_m \angle \beta \)
Then:
\( S = VI^* = V_m I_m \angle (\alpha - \beta) \)

* TRANSFORMERS

** Turns Ratio and Referred Values
- Turns ratio:
  \( a = \frac{N_1}{N_2} = \frac{V_1}{V_2} \)

- Referred impedances:
  \( R_2' = a^2 R_2 \)
  \( X_2' = a^2 X_2 \)
  \( V_2' = a V_2 \)
  \( I_2' = \frac{I_2}{a} \)

** Open-Circuit Test (core parameters)
Given \( V_{oc}, I_{oc}, P_{oc} \):
- Core resistance:
  \( R_c = \frac{V_{oc}^2}{P_{oc}} \)

- Magnetizing reactance:
  \( I_c = \frac{V_{oc}}{R_c} \)
  \( I_m = \sqrt{I_{oc}^2 - I_c^2} \)
  \( X_m = \frac{V_{oc}}{I_m} \)

** Short-Circuit Test (series parameters)
Given \( V_{sc}, I_{sc}, P_{sc} \):
- Equivalent impedance:
  \( Z_{eq} = \frac{V_{sc}}{I_{sc}} \)

- Resistance:
  \( R_{eq} = \frac{P_{sc}}{I_{sc}^2} \)

- Reactance:
  \( X_{eq} = \sqrt{Z_{eq}^2 - R_{eq}^2} \)

** Voltage Regulation
\( \text{VR} = \frac{V_{nl} - V_{fl}}{V_{fl}} \)

** Efficiency
\( \eta = \frac{P_{out}}{P_{out} + P_{cu} + P_{core}} \)

** Transformer EMF Equation (important!)
\( E = 4.44 f N \phi_{\text{max}} \)

* SYNCHRONOUS MACHINES

** Speed and Frequency
- Synchronous speed:
  \( n_s = \frac{120 f}{P} \)

- Mechanical angular:
  \( \omega_m = 2\pi \frac{n}{60} \)

- Electrical angular:
  \( \omega_e = p \cdot \omega_m \)

** Voltage Equation (per-phase)
\( E_f = V_t + I_a(R_a + jX_s) \)

** Power Output
\( P = 3 V_t I_a \cos\theta \)

** Power Angle Curve
\( P = \frac{3EV}{X_s} \sin\delta \)
\( P_{\text{max}} = \frac{3EV}{X_s} \)

** Torque
\( T = \frac{P}{\omega_m} \)

** Tests:
- DC Test → \( R_a \)
- Short Circuit → \( Z_s \)
- Open Circuit → magnetization curve

** Synchronous Reactance
\( X_s = \sqrt{Z_s^2 - R_a^2} \)

** Efficiency & Loading
Armature copper loss:
\( P_{cu} = 3 I_a^2 R_a \)

* INDUCTION MOTORS

** Slip and Speed
Slip:
\( s = \frac{n_s - n}{n_s} \)

Rotor speed:
\( n = (1 - s)n_s \)

Rotor electrical frequency:
\( f_r = s f \)

** Torque Relations
- Developed torque:
  \( T = \frac{P_{conv}}{\omega_m} \)

- Main approximation:
  \( T \propto \frac{sE^2}{R_2^2 + (sX_2)^2} \)

** Maximum Torque
Occurs at:
\( s_{max} = \frac{R_2}{X_2} \)

Magnitude:
\( T_{max} \propto \frac{E^2}{2X_2} \)

** Power Flow Diagram
Stator input → air-gap power \( P_{ag} \) → rotor copper loss \( sP_{ag} \) → converted power \( (1-s)P_{ag} \)

** Important Efficiency Relationship
Mechanical output:
\( P_{out} = (1 - s)P_{ag} - P_{\text{mech loss}} \)

* DC MACHINES

** EMF and Torque
Back EMF:
\( E = k\phi\omega \)

Torque:
\( T = k\phi I_a \)

** Motor Equation
\( V = E + I_a R_a \)

** Generator Equation
\( V = E - I_a R_a \)

** Speed Equation (Shunt Motor)
\( n \approx \frac{V - I_a R_a}{k\phi} \)

** Starting Resistance Calculation
\( I_{start} = \frac{V}{R_a + R_s} \)
Solve:
\( R_s = \frac{V}{I_{start}} - R_a \)

** Power
Mechanical:
\( P_{mech} = T\omega \)

Electrical:
\( P_{elec} = VI \)

* MAGNETIC CIRCUITS

** Reluctance
\( \mathcal{R} = \frac{l}{\mu A} \)

** MMF
\( \mathcal{F} = NI \)

** Flux
\( \phi = \frac{NI}{\mathcal{R}} \)

** Induced Voltage
General form:
\( E = N\frac{d\phi}{dt} \)

Sinusoidal:
\( E = 4.44fN\phi_{max} \)

** Permeability
\( \mu = \mu_0 \mu_r \)

* PER-UNIT SYSTEM

** Base Quantities
\( Z_{base} = \frac{V_{base}^2}{S_{base}} \)
\( I_{base} = \frac{S_{base}}{\sqrt{3} V_{base}} \)

** Per-Unit Conversion
\( x_{pu} = \frac{x_{\text{actual}}}{x_{\text{base}}} \)

** Voltage Bases
For transformers:
Both sides need consistent base values.
