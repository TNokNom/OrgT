#+TITLE: Maximum Likelihood Detection for Binary Transmission over AWGN Channel
This report studies maximum likelihood (ML) detection for binary transmission over an additive white Gaussian noise (AWGN) channel. The theoretical decision rule is derived, and MATLAB simulations are implemented to evaluate the bit error rate (BER) performance for different signal amplitudes. Theoretical and simulated results are compared.


* Section 1.A — Likelihood Functions

*Question:* Determine the probability density functions (PDFs) of the decision variable \( z \) assuming bit ‘1’ or bit ‘0’ is transmitted.

*System Model:*

\[
z = a_i + n_0
\]


\[
n_0 \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \sigma_0^2)
\]

*Gaussian PDF Formula:*
\[
f(x) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi\sigma^2}} e^{- \frac{(x-\mu)^2}{2\sigma^2}}
\]

*Final PDFs:*

\[
f_{z|s_1}(z) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi\sigma_0^2}} 
\exp\left( - \frac{(z-a_1)^2}{2\sigma_0^2} \right)
\]

\[
f_{z|s_2}(z) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi\sigma_0^2}} 
\exp\left( - \frac{(z-a_2)^2}{2\sigma_0^2} \right)
\]


** Section 1.B — Likelihood Ratio Test

*Question:*  Derive the ML likelihood ratio using Bayes’ theorem.

*Bayes' Theorem:*
\[
P(s_i|z) = \frac{f(z|s_i) P(s_i)}{f(z)}
\]

*Likelihood Ratio Definition:*
\[
\Lambda(z) = \frac{P(s_1|z)}{P(s_2|z)}
\]

*Derivation:*

\[
\Lambda(z) = \frac{f(z|s_1) P(s_1)}{f(z|s_2) P(s_2)}
\]


*** Section 1.B{*b*} — Equal Priors

For equally likely bits:

\[
P(s_1) = P(s_2) = \frac{1}{2}
\]

So the likelihood ratio becomes:

\[
\Lambda(z) = \frac{f(z|s_1)}{f(z|s_2)}
\]


*** Section 1.B{*c*} — Closed Form Likelihood Expression

Substitute Gaussian PDFs:

\[
\Lambda(z) = \exp\left(
-\frac{(z-a_1)^2 - (z-a_2)^2}{2\sigma_0^2}
\right)
\]

Expand:

\[
(z-a_2)^2 - (z-a_1)^2 = a_1^2 - a_2^2 + 2z(a_2 - a_1)
\]

Final expression:

\[
\Lambda(z) =
\exp\left(
\frac{a_1^2 - a_2^2}{2\sigma_0^2}
 \frac{z(a_1 - a_2)}{\sigma_0^2}
\right)
\]


** Section 1.C — ML Decision Threshold

*Decision Rule:*
\begin{cases}
1, & \Lambda(z) > 1 \\
0, & \Lambda(z) < 1
\end{cases}

Taking the log:

\[
z > \frac{a_1+a_2}{2}
\]

*Final ML Detector:*

\begin{cases}
1, & z > \frac{a_1+a_2}{2} \\
0, & z < \frac{a_1+a_2}{2}
\end{cases}


* Decision Regions

\[
R_1 = \{ z : z > \frac{a_1+a_2}{2} \}
\]

\[
R_2 = \{ z : z < \frac{a_1+a_2}{2} \}
\]

*** Section 1.D — BER Derivation

Error occurs when:

\[
P_e = Q\left( \frac{a_1 - a_2}{2\sigma_0} \right)
\]

Equivalent form:

\[
P_e = \frac{1}{2} \operatorname{erfc}
\left( \frac{a_1 - a_2}{2\sqrt{2}\sigma_0} \right)
\]


**** Section 1.E — Equal BER Conditions

Equal BER occurs when:

\[
\frac{a_1 - a_2}{\sigma_0} = \text{constant}
\]

***** Section 1.F — Minimum BER with Fixed Energy

Given:

\[
a_1^2 + a_2^2 = \text{constant}
\]

Minimum BER occurs for:

\[
a_1 = -a_2
\]

(antipodal signaling)

*** Section 2.D — Unequal Priors

Given:

\[
P(s_1) = \frac{1}{10}, \quad P(s_2) = \frac{9}{10}
\]

New likelihood:

\[
\Lambda(z) = \frac{f(z|s_1) P(s_1)}{f(z|s_2) P(s_2)}
\]

New threshold:

\[
z > \frac{a_1+a_2}{2}
 \frac{\sigma_0^2}{a_1 - a_2}
\ln\left(\frac{P(s_2)}{P(s_1)}\right)
\]

*Interpretation:*
Threshold moves toward \( s_1 \) because \( s_2 \) is more likely.

ML detection minimizes BER over an AWGN channel. Analytical BER matches simulation, validating theory. Theoretical expressions correctly predict all trends observed from MATLAB.
