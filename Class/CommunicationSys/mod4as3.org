* Part 2.A
Question: *Show orthogonality among sub carriers derive the minimum subcarrier spacing \(f_{\Delta}\) and the minimum total bandwidth.* *Prove that the complex exponentials used as OFDM subcarriers are orthogonal over \(0\le t\le T\), determine the smallest nonzero spacing \(f_{\Delta}\) that achieves orthogonality, and give the minimum occupied bandwidth for \(N_c\) subcarriers.*

FORMULAS USED:

k-th and m-th subcarrier:
\( \phi_k(t)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{T}}e^{j2\pi k f_{\Delta} t},\quad
\phi_m(t)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{T}}e^{j2\pi m f_{\Delta} t} \)

Orthogonality integral (for \(k\neq m\)):
\[
\int_0^T \phi_k(t)\overline{\phi_m(t)}\,dt
= \frac{1}{T}\int_0^T e^{j2\pi (k-m) f_{\Delta} t}\,dt
= \frac{1}{T}\frac{e^{j2\pi (k-m) f_{\Delta}T}-1}{j2\pi (k-m) f_{\Delta}}.
\]

This is zero iff
\( e^{j2\pi (k-m) f_{\Delta}T}=1 \), i.e.
\( (k-m) f_{\Delta} T = \text{integer}. \)

Smallest nonzero spacing (choose integer = 1):
\( \boxed{f_{\Delta}=\frac{1}{T}}. \)

Subcarrier frequencies:
\( f_k = k f_{\Delta},\; k=0,\dots,N_c-1 \)

Highest subcarrier (exact):
\( f_{\max} = (N_c-1)f_{\Delta} = \frac{N_c-1}{T}. \)

Commonly quoted approximate occupied bandwidth:
\( \boxed{B \approx \frac{N_c}{T}}. \)


** Part 2.B
*Question: Compute numerical values for the given system parameters: \(T=10^{-4}\) s and \(N_c=16\). Specifically:*
- *2.B-1: minimum subcarrier spacing \(f_{\Delta}\)*
- *2.B-2: highest subcarrier frequency \(f_{\max}\)*
- *2.B-3: Nyquist sampling constraint → maximum \(t_{\text{step}}\)*
  
*** Formulas Used:

Minimum spacing:
\( f_{\Delta} = \frac{1}{T}. \)

Highest subcarrier (exact):
\( f_{\max} = \frac{N_c-1}{T}. \)

If we treat total bandwidth as \(B \approx \frac{N_c}{T}\), Nyquist sampling requires
\( f_s \ge 2B \Rightarrow t_{\text{step}} \le \frac{1}{2B} = \frac{T}{2N_c}. \)

NUMERICAL RESULTS:

2.B-1:
\( f_{\Delta} = \frac{1}{T} = \frac{1}{10^{-4}} = 10^4\ \text{Hz} = \boxed{10\ \text{kHz}}. \)

2.B-2:
\( f_{\max} = \frac{N_c-1}{T} = \frac{15}{10^{-4}} = 1.5\times10^5\ \text{Hz} = \boxed{150\ \text{kHz}}. \)

2.B-3 (Nyquist max step):
\( t_{\text{step,max}} = \frac{T}{2N_c} = \frac{10^{-4}}{2\cdot16} = \frac{10^{-4}}{32} = 3.125\times10^{-6}\ \text{s} = \boxed{3.125\ \mu\text{s}}. \)
