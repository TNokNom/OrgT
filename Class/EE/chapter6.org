* BJT's Basics
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       b0adc3c5-66a8-4918-8cd4-d5ac6d59853e
:END:
- Bipolar Junction Transistor are a semiconductor device that can amplify current or act as a switch
- There are two types of transistors
  - Each BJT consist of three terminals
    - It has two junctions the *Base-Emitter* and *Base-Collector*
   [[file:../../Pictures/BJT's_Basics/2025-03-22_21-00-40_screenshot.png]]

** Terminals:
*** Emitter
The terminal through which the majority charge carries leave the transistor
*** Base
 - Where voltage is applied. The control terminal
*** Collector
- The terminal through which the majority charge carriers enter the transistor
** NPN
- Comprised of two N-type materials and one P-type Material between them
** PNP
- Comprised of two P-type materials and one N-type material between them

* Modes:
BJTS have three different modes that define their behavior
** Active Mode
- BE is forward biased and the BC is reverse biased

*** NPN
\(V_{B} > V_{E}\) Forward
\(V_{C} > V_{B}\) Reverse

*** PNP
\(V_{E} > V_{B} \) by about .7V
\(v_{B} > v_{C}\)

** Saturation Mode
- BE if forward biased and BC is forward biased

*** NPN
 \(V_{B} > V_{E}\) Forward
\(V_{B} > V_{C}\) Forward

*** PNP
\( V_{E} > V_{B}\)
\(V_{C} > V_{B}\)

** Cut Off Mode
*** NPN
 \(V_{BE} < .7v\)
\(V_{B} < V_{E}\)
\(I_{B} = 0,,,I_{C} = 0 ,,, I_{E}= 0\)

*** PNP
 \(V_{EB} < .7v\)
 \(V_{E} < V_{B}\)
 \(I_{B} = 0,,, I_{C} = 0 ,,, I_{E}= 0\)

* Alpha
- \(\alpha \) (alpha) is the common base current of gain of BJT
 - It tell you what fraction of the emitter current becomes collector current
   - It only applies in active mode when the BE junction if forward biased
     and the BC junction is reverse biased
 \(\alpha = \frac{I_{C}}{I_{E}}\)
\( \alpha =\frac{\beta}{\beta + 1}\)
