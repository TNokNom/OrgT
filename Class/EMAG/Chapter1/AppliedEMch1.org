* Fundamental Forces OF Nature
** Nuclear Force
+ The Strongest of all the four forces
+ But is only seen at the *subatomic* scales
*EX:* Nuclei
** Electromagnetic force
 + Force that exist between all charged particles
 + dominant force in *microscopic* systems
 *EX:* atoms and molecules
+ Strength is on the order of \(10^{-2}\) that of nuclear force
** Weak Force
+ Strength is only \(10^{-14}\) that of nuclear force
+ Primarily involved in the interaction of certain radioactive elementary particles
** Gravitational Force
+ Weakest of the four forces,
+ Has a strength on the order of \(10^{-41}\) weaker than nuclear force
+ Dominant force in *macroscopic* systems
*EX:* Planets in the solar system

* Charge: Electrical Property Of particles
 + A coulomb is the amount of charge accumulated in one second by a current of one ampere
   * 1 coulomb represents the charge on \(6.241 * 10^{18}\) electrons
   * Charge of an electron is -> \(e = 1.602 * 10^{-19} C\)
* Electrical Force
 + It's important to note Coulomb stated three things
   * Two like charges repel one another, whereas two charges of opposite polarity attract
   * The force acts along the line joining the charges
   * Its strength is proportional to the product of the magnitude of the two charges and is
      inversely proportional to the square of the distance between them
