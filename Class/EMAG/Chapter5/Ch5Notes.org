* Lorentz Force
+ Lorentz force combines both electric and magnetic effects into a single expression
  -> \(F = q(E + v \times B)\)

* Magnetic Force
+ The direction of magnetic force exerted on a charged particle moving in a magnetic field
   is perpendicular to both (*B*) and (*u*) and depends on the charge polarity
+ \(F_{m} = qu \times B\)
 * Is stating that if a charge moves in magnetic space there is a force on that charge

** Electromagnetic (Lorentz) Force
+ \( F = F_{e}+ F_{m} = qE + qu \times B = q(E + u \times B)\)
  * Combination of the electric and magnetic force hence the name

* Magnetic Force On A Current Element
+ For a closed circuit of contour (*C*) carrying a current (*I*) the total magnetic force is
  + \(F_{m} = I  \oint_{C}dl \times B \)
    * If the closed wire resides in a uniform external magnetic field (*B*) then it can be
       taken outside the integral
       + \(F_{m} = I (\oint_{C}dl) \times B = 0 \)
* Torque
+ Torque is \( T = d \times F\) where (*d*) is the moment arm and (*F*) is the force and (*T*) is the torque
