** Device Structure
- Composed of semiconductor materials
- Includes source, drain, gate, and substrate
- Utilizes electric fields for operation

** Zero Gate Operation
- Device is off when no voltage is applied
- Channel is devoid of charge carriers
- Drain current is minimal

** Channel Creation
-> Fig 5.2
- Downward electric field from G to the substrate
- Draws electrons out of the substrate 
- Creates a depletion region

*** Threshold Voltage
- \(V_{th}\) is the minimum voltage to attract electrons
- Defines the onset of conduction in a n-type channel
  - Critical parameter for device operation

*** Overside Voltage
- \( V_{ov} = V_{GS} - V_{th}\)
- Overdrive voltage, often denoted as \( V_{ov} \), is the difference between the gate-source voltage (\( V_{GS} \)) and the threshold voltage (\( V_{th} \)) of a MOSFET.
- Represents excess voltage beyond \(V_{th}\)
- Influences channel conductivity


** Small Drain Source Voltage
- Device acts as a voltage-controlled resistor
- \(V_{GS}\) is the control voltage
- Inverse slope represents resistance

** Larger Drain Source Voltage
- \(V_{GS}\) is held constant to study \(V_{DS}\)
- Voltage decreases from source to drain
- Channel width narrows along the device length

** Pinch Off and current saturation
- Condition where the channel becomes fully depleted
- Results in saturation of drain current
- Important for analog applications

* NMOS

** Symbol
- Represents n-channel MOSFET
- Indicates source, drain, and gate connections
- Often depicted in circuit diagrams

** Cutoff Region
- Device is off, no current flows
- Occurs when \(V_{GS} < V_{th}\)
- Resistance is extremely high

** Triode Region
- Device operates as a linear resistor
- Drain current increases with \(V_{DS}\)
- Depends on \(V_{GS}\) and channel length

** Saturation Region
 - Device reaches maximum current flow
 - Occurs when \(V_{DS}\) exceeds pinch-off voltage
 - \(i_{d}\) becomes relatively constant

** Output Characteristics
 - Curves illustrate \(I_{D}\) vs. \(V_{DS}\)
 - Shows device behavior across regions
 - Useful for understanding performance

** Symbol
 - Represents p-channel MOSFET
 - Shows reversed connections compared to NMOS
 - Important for CMOS technology

** Cutoff Region
 - Device is off, no current flows
- Occurs when \(V_{GS} > V_{th}\)
 - Highly resistive condition

** Triode Region
 - Operates as a variable resistor
 - Drain current depends on \(V_{DS}\) and \(V_{GS}\)
 - Useful for amplification purposes

** Saturation Region
 - Current reaches a maximum value
 - \(V_{DS}\) exceeds pinch-off voltage
 - Behavior similar to NMOS saturation

** Output Characteristics
 - Demonstrates \(I_{D}\) vs. \(V_{DS}\) for PMOS
 - Key for analyzing device performance
 - Exhibits similar trends to NMOS but in inverse direction
