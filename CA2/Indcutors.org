
#+Title: Transformers Continued
* Mutual Inductance
- Property of electric circuits in which a time-varying current in one
  inductor results in a voltage across a second inductor
- Caused by flux linkage between the two inductors
- Denoted as M
- Units: Henries (H)


** Mutual Inductance *Dot Convention*
- Current enetering the dotted terminal of one coil produces a voltage
  that is sensed positively at the dotted terminal of the second coil
